The JSON uses `suite`, `seed`, `checks`, and `elapsed_seconds`, plus exact manifest hash/commit, dtype, tolerances and evidence scope. Each check has the requested ID, lineage, scope, status, metrics, source references and interpretation. Per-check exceptions are retained as failed records and do not suppress later checks. Any unexpected identity, numerical or implementation failure makes the process exit nonzero. Boundary checks are explicitly described as **passed boundary reproduction**, never as the success of the invalid extrapolation. No check is designed to return inconclusive silently; uncertainty in a numerical fixture surfaces as a failed diagnostic requiring root review.

A21 distinguishes coefficient-space spectral quantities from energy. The retained historical metrics `common_ledger_worst_leakage` and `common_envelope_tail_certificate` are unscaled coefficients, also emitted with explicit `_coefficient` names. The metrics `common_ledger_worst_scaled_energy_bound` and `common_envelope_tail_scaled_energy_bound` multiply the respective coefficients by `pairwise_radius**2`; here R=1.4, so the scale factor is 1.96. The first is the exact worst residual energy for the chosen common ledger; the second is its conservative envelope energy certificate. This reporting clarification changes no fixture, predicate or mathematical prediction. The exact pre-clarification script is preserved as `checkpoints/check_algebra.unscaled-coefficients-v1.py` (SHA256 `fd7da3dbce525e674a8d965eb61d324e186f11c8c1e47b11619469c56acc54ee`).

Tolerance policy: norm errors ≤2e−10+2e−10·scale for ordinary identities; complete-Jacobian finite-difference errors ≤2e−7·max(1, operator norm) at three steps. Scalar bounded-optimizer action comparison has 2e−7 tolerance. The cancellation-prone local nonlinear curvature boundary has 1e−5 tolerance. SVD rank threshold is 1e−11·max(1, leading singular value); exact graph edges are determined on controlled integer/explicit-epsilon fixtures, not on unqualified floating data.

Coverage limits are consequential. These finite constructed diagnostics do not prove the universal theorems. A01's random credit family can require full width; only the zero-coupling controlled case demonstrates a smaller width. A09 checks robust dimensional reduction but does not solve arbitrary many-mode robust QCQPs. A17 tests a rank-one overwrite and its zero-row insufficient-budget tail; it does not enumerate every higher-rank ledger budget. A19 assumes a shared reducing carrier and stores its complement action explicitly. A21 tests a finite family and one fixed event; it does not construct a lawful prefix branch/radius certificate or a recurrent side-code transition. The only exact Grassmann robust optimum reproduced is the two-dimensional complementary-world example. These restrictions must stay visible if results are summarized.
