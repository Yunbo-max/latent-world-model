## Execution blockers and assumptions that need evidence

The shared bottleneck is not small-matrix arithmetic. Many objects assume a suffix, future covector/Gramian, lawful branch family or global derivative envelope known before a write. Retrospective construction is a valid diagnostic but not a deployed causal certificate. Native text gives one factual suffix per prefix; it does not directly provide conditional means, unchosen actions or semantic validity. Complete-state feature/gate/query/routing/updater feedback can invalidate the frozen affine laws even with a bounded sigmoid gate. Exact real-valued one-shot side coordinates do not specify a stable finite-bit code or a constant-width transition for subsequent destructive writes.

For CPU feasibility, dimensions, path depth and field cardinality must be bounded: exhaustive R19 codes grow as q^n, continuation trees grow exponentially, full Hessian/Jacobian objects grow quadratically or worse, and nonconvex Grassmann/robust coding problems need small independently checkable instances. Naive high-dimensional empirical Wasserstein estimation cannot be replaced by projected/entropic distance without changing the theorem. PSD envelope/eigentruncation is not generally the robust subspace optimum. Finite-frame HT normalization and oracle suffix metrics cannot become causal one-pass rules merely by running them on CPU.

Source boundary issue: `LYAPUNOV_OBLIQUE_METRIC.md` states the SPD certificate iff 0<c<=2 for A=I-wr^T without declaring w,r nonzero. Identity cases w=0 or r=0 have c=0 and trivially admit SPD certificates. Qualify that statement to a nontrivial update or handle identity cases. This is a source assumption omission, not evidence that its nontrivial oblique construction fails.
