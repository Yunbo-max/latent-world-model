For a fixed coordinate carrier, coordinate-diagonal decay and independently closed complement, a chunk stores \(Q^\perp:O(d)\), \(K:O(r^2)\) and \(H:O(rm)\). The stated naive combine \(O(d+r^3+r^2m)\) and apply \(O(dm+r^2m)\) costs are valid only for that interface. They are not end-to-end costs.

The total ledger must also charge per-token construction of \(K_t,h_t\), dense-basis storage and projection, basis discovery and maintenance, backward work, materialization, kernel launch, memory traffic, precision and any recompression. A general dense complement can restore quadratic state and cubic combine cost. Martin–Cundy's released CUDA only supplies the diagonal/elementwise operational control; it does not fill the missing dense-core implementation or runtime evidence.

A lawful carrier must be fixed after training, prefix-causal with enough retained history, or discovered offline from an already supplied chunk with all discovery/storage/reconstruction costs charged. It cannot use an unseen autoregressive suffix or recover previously discarded coordinates for free. Shared geometry provides no valid/obsolete fact label.

bAbI, LAMBADA, RULER, LongMemEval and DeltaProduct endpoints can measure downstream behavior but do not natively expose the simultaneous generator, exact token/chunk ranks, a prefix-known carrier, recompression/backward errors, semantic validity or matched dense-core kernel traffic. Added numerical-rank instrumentation and synthetic fixtures are diagnostics or theorem/software verification, not native scientific measurement.

Required same-information controls are: the identical direct \(r\)-dimensional affine recurrence/scan with the same complement; sequential exact joint flow; full dense affine composition; ordered Delta/WY; grouped KDA; R11 lazy factors plus QR/SVD recompression; DeltaProduct; and matched-tolerance DPR1/exponential action.

Unclosed gaps include evidence that a small prefix-known carrier naturally exists and remains stable, its dimension distribution, violation effects, an independent scientific reason for the simultaneous generator, a matched dense-core implementation, a link from Frobenius state error to semantic loss, full-network state dependence and an observable retain/revise target.

**Disposition:** no candidate promotion; park R16 after attempt 3/3 with zero active/admitted/selected delta and unknown empirical effect.
