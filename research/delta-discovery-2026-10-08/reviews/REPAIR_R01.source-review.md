## Substantive bounded judgments

**A: fix the hypothesis rather than discard the question.** The meaningful patch is common residual/value-span closure while retaining the scalar-gate derivative. Left multiplication preserves a fixed right span regardless of whether successive left factors commute. The out-of-span component can feed back through the gate into the in-span component; R01's leakage recurrence keeps that interaction. This is a useful conditional characterization of the original failure and a legitimate repaired lead/control. It does not establish an unexplored method: fixed-span invariant subspaces are familiar, and enforcing full `S=ZV^T` with values in V reduces to an ordinary smaller value-width Delta model. Same capacity bytes and readout/feature access must be compared. The nontrivial remaining question is whether a causal useful geometry and affordable feedback bound exist without sacrificing task-relevant capacity.

**B: repair the decision criterion.** Certifying the scalar credit rather than reconstructing every tangent entry is scientifically relevant. A low relative tangent error alone can reverse a tiny credit, whereas a high-rank error can be irrelevant when orthogonal to the costate. The exact residual-to-credit identity and robust finite-step majorizer explain both conditions. Adjoint weighting, smooth majorization and uncertainty-aware abstention are established mathematical principles. The possible residual contribution is a Delta-specific causal and cost-effective certificate, which is not supplied or claimed here. No general retention, factual-validity, updater-improvement or RSI result follows from one teacher-forced suffix step.

**Costs remain material.** Full nominal state, dense gate gradient/projection, full feature/updater derivatives, reverse costates, suffix activations/replay, basis maintenance and interval-wide curvature certification can consume the apparent sensitivity saving. An empirical or learned estimate of a bound cannot be silently substituted for a legal deterministic certificate. The file's final K/Q paragraph now constructs an actual dominating upper-bound sequence, but obtaining the interval derivative suprema affordably remains open. When the bounds are too loose, baseline preservation is a valid mathematical outcome; it is not evidence of practical learning advantage.
