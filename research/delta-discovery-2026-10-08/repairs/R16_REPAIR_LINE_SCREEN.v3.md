## Decision

The third attempt closes v2's earliest mathematical premise. A common reducing carrier gives an exact fixed-width active core together with an independently closed and charged complement action; the full scan is compact only when the complement is also cheap. Varying one-dimensional token cores can require full dimension over a chunk. The necessity statement is restricted to the fixed two-sided common-carrier representation; it does not exclude other finite operator algebras, different left/right carriers, triangular closures, cancellations, or the full dense affine scan. This distinction is useful for debugging claims that small per-token active rank implies compact sequence parallelism.

It does not create a new updater. The positive construction is exactly a direct \(r\)-dimensional affine recurrence with the same carrier and source; the general factor ledger is generic ordered low-rank maintenance; the endpoint is still normalized inverse-metric Delta; the simultaneous generator has no independent natural justification; retain/revise validity remains unidentified; and no native task measures the internal mechanism under matched total cost.

R16 is therefore parked after substantive attempt 3/3 as a conditional theorem/control with zero active, scientifically admitted, or selected candidates. Reopen only with a lawful natural shared carrier or another exact closed representation that beats the direct same-information controls at matched total cost, together with independent evidence for the simultaneous generator or update decision.
