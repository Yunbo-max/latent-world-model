## Decision

R19 performs the first substantive revision of the selected switching line. The patch does not infer which fact is obsolete. It conditions on a lawful protection-mode schedule and asks the narrower dynamical question: when can a certificate proved in one protected quotient survive a mode change?

The exact obstruction is anticipated before the derivation: if the old transverse energy has a kernel direction that the new mode starts measuring, no finite multiplicative jump factor can exist unless the reset annihilates/transfers that direction or an additional released-coordinate term is charged. This is not evidence that release is always harmful. It identifies the missing state/information obligation and preserves R08's option to release after adequate evidence.
