## 1. Original problem, failure type, and patch

The useful v2 question survives: can the value-side tangent of a Delta memory be replaced by a low-width exact statistic for the future losses that actually matter? The failed step was using the raw union of local gate and loss row spaces as though it were the smallest useful span. That union is a safe local envelope under v2's closure assumptions, but it ignores cancellation and zero coupling after future transport.

Failure type: **target/construction mismatch plus closest-work collision**, not a false v2 theorem. The patch is to define the target first—a fixed finite family of future scalar credits along a frozen/teacher-forced path—and derive the minimal right quotient for exactly those credits by adjoint transport.

## 2. Formal object and assumptions

Let `X_j in R^{d_k x d_v}` be a perturbation of the key-by-value memory at time `j`. Along one declared frozen path, assume the affine tangent recurrence

\[
X_{j+1}=J_j[X_j]+B_j,
\qquad
J_j[X]=A_jX+K_j\langle G_j,X\rangle_F,
\tag{1}
\]

where `A_j in R^{d_k x d_k}`, `K_j,G_j in R^{d_k x d_v}`, and every `B_j` is fixed with respect to the original focal perturbation `X_t` and any omitted recurrent-state perturbation on this declared affine path. If another recurrent component carries `X_t` into `B_j`, it must instead be included in the joint state and Jacobian. For scalar-gated Delta, `K_j=k_je_j^T`; `G_j` is the derivative of the gate contribution with respect to the Delta-memory perturbation on the declared frozen path, not the full joint-state derivative. All inner products are Frobenius inner products.

At time `s`, requested scalar credit `m` has state-dependent part

\[
z_{s,m}(X_s)=\langle C_{s,m},X_s\rangle_F,
\qquad C_{s,m}\in R^{d_k\times d_v}.
\tag{2}
\]

Known affine contributions induced by the `B_j` terms can be accumulated separately; they do not change which linear functionals of the focal `X_t` must be retained. The theorem below is exact only for the declared path, tangent maps, and finite credit family.

For a full-column-rank right map, replacing its columns by an orthonormal basis does not change the retained column space, so take without loss of generality `W in R^{d_v x r}` with orthonormal columns. The case `r=0` uses the empty matrix and retains no coordinates. Define the right quotient

\[
L_W(X)=XW.
\tag{3}
\]

## 3. Exact adjoint and transported covectors

For arbitrary `X,Y in R^{d_k x d_v}`,

\[
\begin{aligned}
\langle Y,J_j[X]\rangle_F
&=\langle Y,A_jX\rangle_F
  +\langle Y,K_j\rangle_F\langle G_j,X\rangle_F\\
&=\left\langle A_j^TY+G_j\langle K_j,Y\rangle_F, X\right\rangle_F.
\end{aligned}
\]

Hence

\[
J_j^*[Y]=A_j^TY+G_j\langle K_j,Y\rangle_F.
\tag{4}
\]

For `t<s`, define each transported costate unambiguously by the backward recursion

\[
D_{s,s,m}=C_{s,m},
\qquad
D_{j,s,m}=J_j^*[D_{j+1,s,m}],\quad j=s-1,\ldots,t.
\tag{5}
\]

Equivalently, `D_{t,s,m}=J_t^*\circ J_{t+1}^*\circ\cdots\circ J_{s-1}^*[C_{s,m}]`, where the rightmost adjoint acts first. Repeated use of the adjoint identity gives

\[
\left\langle C_{s,m},J_{s-1}\circ\cdots\circ J_t[X_t]\right\rangle_F
=\langle D_{t,s,m},X_t\rangle_F.
\tag{6}
\]

Thus each future scalar credit is exactly a present-time linear functional with covector `D_{t,s,m}`.

## 4. Minimal-width theorem within right quotients

### Lemma

For orthonormal `W`, the statistic `XW` determines `\langle D,X\rangle_F` for every `X` iff

\[
D=DWW^T,
\tag{7}
\]

equivalently `row(D) subseteq range(W)`.

*Proof.* If (7) holds, `\langle D,X\rangle_F=\langle DW,XW\rangle_F`. Conversely, if `D(I-WW^T) != 0`, choose `X=D(I-WW^T)`. Then `XW=0` but

\[
\langle D,X\rangle_F=\|D(I-WW^T)\|_F^2>0,
\]

so no function of `XW` determines the credit. QED.

### Theorem

For a declared family `S_t` of future scalar credits, with exactness required for every ambient `X_t in R^{d_k x d_v}`, the minimum width among exact right quotients is

\[
r_t^*=\dim\operatorname{span}\{\operatorname{row}(D_{t,s,m}):(s,m)\in S_t\}.
\tag{8}
\]

Any orthonormal basis `W_t` of this row span is sufficient, and the lemma proves that no smaller right quotient can be sufficient.

The same result can be generated by defining the declared per-time covector subspace

\[
\mathcal C_j=\operatorname{span}\{C_{j,m}:(j,m)\in S_t\},
\tag{9a}
\]

with `\mathcal C_j={0}` when no credit is declared at `j`, and using the backward matrix-subspace recursion

\[
\mathcal D_H=\operatorname{span}\mathcal C_H,
\qquad
\mathcal D_j=\operatorname{span}\bigl(\mathcal C_j\cup J_j^*(\mathcal D_{j+1})\bigr),
\tag{9}
\]

followed by taking the joint row span of all matrices in `mathcal D_j`. Equation (9) is a full-matrix costate construction; the value-side span alone is generally not closed enough to compute the next span. If all declared covectors are zero then `r_t^*=0`, consistent with the empty quotient.

The ambient-state qualification matters. If exactness is required only on a proper reachable tangent subspace, the annihilator of that subspace can make a smaller quotient sufficient; equation (8) is then an upper bound unless the corresponding restricted dual problem is solved.

## 5. Relation to the v2 envelope

Equation (4) implies

\[
\operatorname{row}(J_j^*Y)
\subseteq
\operatorname{span}\bigl(\operatorname{row}(Y),\operatorname{row}(G_j)\bigr),
\tag{10}
\]

and the `G_j` term vanishes whenever `\langle K_j,Y\rangle_F=0`. Therefore the raw v2 union of all declared future `row(C_{j,m})` and local `row(G_j)` is an upper envelope for this declared family, while transported closure can be strictly smaller through zero coupling, rank loss, or cancellation.

This does **not** give a cheap value-only online recursion: evaluating `A_j^TY` and `\langle K_j,Y\rangle_F` generally requires full costate matrices or an additional proved factorization. It also requires the future path/credit family, so it is naturally an offline audit, teacher-forced training calculation, or oracle diagnostic.

## 6. Old counterexample recheck and strengthened no-go

The v2 counterexample—two states with identical `XW` but different discarded components—still applies whenever a transported covector has a row outside `range(W)`. The patch does not remove it; equation (8) chooses the smallest `W` that excludes precisely those hidden directions for the declared family.

More strongly, no prefix-only rule can guarantee a nontrivial exact right quotient against the **formal suffix/credit class that permits arbitrary rank-one terminal covectors and ambient perturbations**. At time `t`, take an identity continuation (`A_j=I`, zero gate derivative, hence `J_j=I`) and choose one future loss covector `C_{s,m}=u a^T`, with nonzero `u` and arbitrary unit `a in R^{d_v}`. Then `D_{t,s,m}=C_{s,m}`. For any prefix-chosen rank-`r<d_v` `W`, select `a perpendicular to range(W)`. The lemma supplies two ambient perturbations with identical quotient and different credit. Across this formal class, exactness therefore forces `r=d_v`. This does not prove that every `a` or witness perturbation is realizable by a lawful language-model suffix/native loss and reachable tangent; such realizability needs a separate model/task argument.

The no-go is conditional: it does not rule out compression under a declared, externally justified continuation-support law whose credit covectors occupy a proper value subspace.

## 7. Computable construction, prediction, and falsifier

Offline construction for a fixed path and credit set:

1. obtain each terminal `C_{s,m}` from the actual scalar objective;
2. propagate it backward with (4), preserving full matrix costates;
3. stack bases for all transported row spaces;
4. compute an orthonormal basis by rank-revealing QR/SVD; and
5. retain `X_tW_t`, plus separately accumulated affine credit constants.

Distinctive prediction: whenever many local `G_j` directions have zero transported coupling `\langle K_j,D_{j+1,s,m}\rangle_F` or collapse under `A_j^T`, measured transported rank (8) is smaller than the raw v2 envelope while reproducing the declared scalar credits to numerical tolerance.

Falsifiers:

- any two `X_t` with the same `X_tW_t` but different declared credits disproves the implementation of the theorem;
- transported rank near `d_v` removes the intended compression value;
- a same-information direct reverse VJP or direct credit predictor matching accuracy at lower state/time cost removes practical value;
- a lawful prefix-checkable support rule that produces the same span more cheaply would supersede this construction.

## 8. Information, state, and computation cost

The exact audit needs future terminal covectors and the realized/frozen Jacobian path. It is not a causal deployment statistic. With `M` distinct credits, naively retaining dense costates costs `O(M d_k d_v)` state. Applying a dense `A_j^T` to one costate costs `O(d_k^2 d_v)` in the worst case, in addition to the rank-one correction; stacked rank-revealing QR/SVD adds further cost and may reach full `d_v` width. Structure can reduce these costs only if separately proved. Full nonlinear recurrence requires the actual joint-state VJP, not the frozen-path Delta-only surrogate.

For a single scalar objective, ordinary reverse-mode VJP/BPTT with checkpointing is the strongest simple computational control. For many fixed objectives, shared basis construction may amortize, but this is an empirical cost claim and remains untested.

## 9. Closest work and residual contribution

The core construction is an observability/adjoint quotient: it is the dual of retaining precisely the state directions distinguished by future outputs. Pappas-style linear bisimulation, nonlinear/control bisimulation, observability Gramians, exact lumping (including CLUE), predictive-state/fiber formulations, and this packet's R18 joint recursive quotient already cover the general mechanism. R18 is broader in state/output scope because it treats the full joint recurrent state and output family rather than only a Delta value-side right quotient; R01 separately gives the constrained-class minimum-width formula.

Residual contribution: an exact Delta specialization that (i) replaces the v2 raw-union heuristic by transported covectors, (ii) gives the closed-form minimal right width (8), and (iii) proves the same-prefix full-width boundary. It is useful theory/control, but current evidence does not support a distinct new method or architecture.
