## Selection rationale

Line A changes the missing information premise without deleting the old counterexamples. It is the smallest lawful repair: the set-valued tree is measurable from the current prefix, while the realized future branch is not revealed early. The resulting theorem is exact for affine complete-state systems and all branches in the declared tree. It also produces a sharp negative result: two same-prefix futures that observe complementary directions can make the common invisible subspace trivial even though each oracle branch admits compression.

The repair is not a new updater. The recursion is a finite-tree form of robust observability/common invariant-subspace refinement, and the quotient/side-ledger factorization is a direct sufficient-coordinate control. Its value is a Delta-specific debugging theorem separating:

1. a lawful prefix certificate;
2. an oracle realized-branch certificate;
3. a probabilistic coverage claim;
4. downstream endpoint performance.
