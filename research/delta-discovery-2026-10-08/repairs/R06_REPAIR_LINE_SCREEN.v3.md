## Three bounded continuations

1. **Architecturally enforced invertibility margin — selected.** Restrict every future rank-one Delta factor by a declared singular-value floor. This supplies an exact two-sided product bound without observing future keys, and exposes a necessary preservation–correction tradeoff because the same scalar controls both old-state survival and current-key residual reduction.
2. **Learn the lower continuation ratio from logged suffixes — not selected.** A predictor of `Z_i/X_i` is useful empirically, but without simultaneous lower coverage under the deployed continuation law it does not repair the certificate. It also returns to generic learned influence proposals and would require a new protocol unavailable in native benchmarks.
3. **Replace the frozen product by the complete network Jacobian — not selected here.** This is mathematically legitimate but repeats R18/R01 full-state observability and transported-credit obligations. A lower singular bound for the complete Jacobian is strictly stronger and cannot be inferred from the Delta memory block alone.

## Decision

Proceed with line 1 for the third and final attempt. The admissible result is a sharp conditional theorem/control: an enforced survival margin makes the v2 competitive factor finite, but the certificate decays multiplicatively with horizon and forbids exact one-step correction. It becomes a candidate only if the restriction yields a Delta-specific same-budget advantage over ordinary damped/invertible residual updates and learned influence sampling; no such evidence is assumed.
