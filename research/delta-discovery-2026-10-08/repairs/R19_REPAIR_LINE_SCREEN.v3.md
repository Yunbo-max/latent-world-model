## Repair outcome

For hidden coordinate `z in F_q^n`, post-write side information `Az`, and branch targets `B_m z+b_m`, define

\[
r=\operatorname{rank}([A;B_1;\ldots;B_M])-\operatorname{rank}(A).
\]

Every `A`-fiber splits into cosets of `ker A intersect ker(stack B_m)`. Distinct cosets are mutually adjacent, so the graph is complete `q^r`-partite inside each fiber and its exact chromatic number is `q^r`. A complement-row syndrome `Cz` with `r` symbols attains the bound. A clique of size `q^r` proves that nonlinear or zero-error randomized encoders cannot use fewer messages.

The code is polynomial-time constructible and can beat a naive concatenation of branch targets. However, a row-reduced direct signature that quotients information already visible in `Az` has exactly the same `r`-symbol state. R18's joint-row ledger is the same strong control. Thus the requested matched-information advantage is absent.
