The final repair is `R17_RESIDUAL_SCALED_SPECTRAL_CERTIFICATE.v3.md`. It fixes a real action-level target mismatch and gives a sharp gate conditional on an already-issued lawful certificate, but it does not construct the certificate or close the original arbitrary-pair quotient problem. The mechanism remains ordinary residual-aware gating under a compressed observability metric. A directly calibrated scalar score for the current `(prefix,k)` and the closed-form ordinary quadratic gate are mandatory controls. Any apparent float-count saving over a ledger is unresolved under matched bits/metadata and is also explained by weaker functionality: the certificate throttles or abstains instead of preserving protected values through the write.

R17 is therefore parked and closed after attempt 3/3. Candidate, scientific-admission and selection increments remain zero; empirical effect is unknown. Reopening requires new causal information, a deployed prefix-valid one-sided certificate with a matched-cost advantage over both dense gating and direct coordinates, and a native mechanism scorer. Hyperparameter, rank, sketch and threshold variants do not qualify.
