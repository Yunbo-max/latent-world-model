## Scope and lineage

This screen selects the third and final substantive R20 repair without modifying the bytes of v1 or v2. V1 proved that a genuinely nonseparable quadratic can require a higher-rank exact Delta edit. V2 replaced an oracle dense operator by a prefix-causal low-rank surrogate and derived its exact projected-Woodbury action. The remaining earliest premise was prefix-to-future transfer.

## Selected operation

Use generalized eigenvalue whitening and the Kantorovich product inequality on the affine feasible set. This yields the sharp factor

\[
\frac{(M+m)^2}{4Mm},
\]

not the loose \(M/m\). Then construct two identical-prefix futures, one satisfying and one violating the sandwich, to test whether the premise can be verified at action time.

## Outcome

The mathematical implication is repaired under a precisely stated homogeneous-quadratic spectral premise on \(L_e=\operatorname{span}(\mathcal C_e)\). The deployment premise is not repaired: arbitrary future continuations are observationally indistinguishable from the prefix. The robust symmetric uncertainty set also returns the exact same v2 action, so it does not create a distinct updater.

**Final screen result:** preserve R20 v3 as a conditional theorem/no-go control; exhaust the lineage after attempt 3; candidate delta 0; empirical status unknown.
