### SDM discrepancy is algebraic, not an experiment

For two selected rows and nonzero k1,k2, the aggregate Delta correction to row1 contains −beta*k1*k2*Mtilde[2]. Literal row-local Eq4 has no Mtilde[2] term. Hence the two rules differ generically. Agreement when only one unit-weight row is selected does not establish dense equivalence. Author AppendixA/source support the coupled construction; clarification of main-text shorthand remains open. This observation is a control audit, not a new method or reported failure of trained SDM.

### Symbolic resource feasibility, not a dispatch

One dense head stores d_k*d_v numbers and does O(d_k*d_v) recurrent work/token. m microsteps add O(m*d_k*d_v) work while retaining that matrix; learned feature projections and training activations also cost. Full covariance/protection matrix adds O(d_k²) state, dense solve potentially O(d_k³); rank-r protection basis O(d_k*r), applying projection O(d_k*r), basis maintenance/release not free. SDM table O(N*d_v), read/write O((W+R)*d_v), product-key scoring O(d_model*sqrt(N)+W²+R²), plus chunk interactions/solves/backward buffers; sparse access does not imply tiny GPU memory. A fullhistory reader has sequence-dependentKV cost, a recurrent reader still ingests allTtokens, and BM25 stores/reads text/index onCPU.

These costs are symbolic only. User0.1B/1B pretraining-target-token budget perarm/seed and2080Ti are feasibility background, not proof any comparator fits/runs. Native LongMemEval semantic judging is a concrete unresolved access condition; no paidAPI/local70B workaround is authorized. This excludes that native score from an execution-ready recommendation, while leaving its task/formula evidence useful for candidate reasoning.
